---
title: Measure
description: Read the size of a face, edge or body, or the distance and angle between two of them.
---

**Measure** reads sizes without changing the model. Key: [[I]]. It is in the **Inspect** group.

## Use it

1. Press [[I]] for **Measure**.
2. Click a **face**, an **edge** or a **body** (pick bodies in the browser). The result appears in a panel in the top-right corner of the viewport.
3. Click a **second** thing to measure between them.
4. Keep clicking: every click starts a new measurement. [[Esc]] finishes.

## What you see

For **one** item: its type (for example *Flat face*), area, perimeter, size along each axis and its normal direction.

For **two**: the **shortest distance** and the **angle** between them, followed by the details of each.

![Measuring the angle and distance between two faces.](shot:measure-panel)

Press **Copy** in the panel to copy the numbers.

:::tip
Use Measure to check a wall before you print: click the inside and outside faces, and read the distance.
:::

Measure never adds a feature to the timeline.
