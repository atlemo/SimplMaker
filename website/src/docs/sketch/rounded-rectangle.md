---
title: Rounded Rectangle
description: Draw a rectangle with all four corners rounded by one radius, and change the size and radius with dimensions.
---

A **rounded rectangle** is a rectangle with its four corners rounded by the same radius. It is one of the three ways of drawing a rectangle. Press [[R]] to pick the Rectangle, then press it again for the **Center** rectangle, and once more for **Rounded**.

| Mode | Click |
|---|---|
| **2-Point** | Two opposite corners |
| **Center** | The centre, then a corner |
| **Rounded** | Two opposite corners, with the corners rounded |

## Draw one

1. Press [[R]] until the toolbar says **Rounded**.
2. Type the **Radius** in the field beside the pointer, or leave it at the last value you used. It starts at 5 mm. You can type it before the first click or after it.
3. Click two opposite corners. The rectangle is drawn with its corners rounded.

After the first click, the **Width**, **Height** and **Radius** fields are all there. Press [[Tab]] to go from one to the next, and [[Return]] to place the shape from what you typed.

## Size and radius

- **Width** and **Height** are the size of the rectangle as if its corners were square. The box you click is that size, so the rounded corners cut into it without changing the size you typed.
- The **radius** is one dimension, on the first corner. The other three corners are set equal to it, so changing the radius changes all four at once.
- Change any of them later by editing its dimension, as described in [Dimensions](doc:sketch/dimensions).

:::note
The radius can be no more than half the shorter side. If it is bigger, SimplMaker says so and draws nothing. Make the radius smaller, or the rectangle bigger.
:::
