## The trial

SimplMaker is free to try for **14 days**, with every feature. The trial starts the first time you open the app, and needs no account.

A banner in the window shows how long is left. When it ends, you can still **open** and **export** your parts, but you can't change them until you enter a licence key. A banner then says *Editing is off*.

The trial can't be extended by changing the clock: SimplMaker notices if the date is set back.

## Buy a licence

Press **Buy…** in the banner, or choose **SimplMaker → Buy SimplMaker…**. Checkout is handled by Polar, which sends you a licence key by email. See the [pricing page](/pricing/).

## Activate it

1. Choose **SimplMaker → License…** (or press **Enter License…** in the banner).
2. Paste the key from your email and press **Activate**.

The window then shows the end of your key and says *Licensed*. There is no account and no sign-in.

## Two Macs

One key works on **two Macs**. To free an activation, open **SimplMaker → License…** on that Mac and press **Deactivate This Mac**. Then activate on the new one. Deactivate first if you are selling or wiping a Mac.

## Checks and offline use

About **once a week** while licensed, SimplMaker asks the licence server whether the key is still valid. If you are offline it keeps working, and only needs to reach the server again after **30 days**. It tells you before that happens.

Your parts are never sent anywhere. The check sends only the key, the activation and the name of your Mac.

## Which versions a key covers

A key covers **one major version**: every 1.x update, for example. It never expires. A new major version is a separate purchase. A key for another major version is turned away with a message.

## Problems

- *Key not accepted*: check it is for this major version, and that you copied all of it with no spaces.
- *Cannot be activated*: it may already be on two Macs. Deactivate one, or [contact support](/support/).
- Lost your key: write to support from the email you bought with.
