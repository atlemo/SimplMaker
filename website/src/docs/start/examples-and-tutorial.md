## The welcome window

SimplMaker opens a welcome window, like Xcode's.

![The welcome window.](shot:welcome-window)

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Make your first part** | Starts the guided tutorial |
| **Create a new part** | Opens an empty part |
| **Open the demo part** | Opens a plate with a hole, fillets and a chamfer |
| **Open an existing part** | Opens a `.simplmaker` file |

Your recent parts are listed on the right. Double-click one, or select it and press [[Return]].

To stop it appearing at launch, untick **Show this window when SimplMaker launches**, or turn it off in **Settings → General**. **Window → Welcome to SimplMaker** ([[⇧⌘1]]) brings it back.

## The tutorial: a phone stand

A guided tutorial of about five minutes. A card at the bottom of the view walks you through making a phone stand you can print:

- five named parameters
- a sketch and an extrusion for the base
- a construction plane that leans by an angle, with a second sketch and extrusion on it for the backrest
- changing `width` at the end, to turn the phone stand into a tablet stand

The card follows what you actually do. It moves on when the part shows a step is done, steps back if you undo it, and tells you when something is off, such as a rectangle drawn with plain numbers instead of parameters, or a backrest that isn't joined to the base.

- The **‹** button goes back a step and undoes what you did in it.
- **Do it for me** performs the current step the way the interface would, so you can see how.
- **Leave tutorial** (or the ✕) closes the card.
- **Help → Resume Tutorial** brings it back.

What to use next pulses gently in the toolbar.

## Example parts

**File → Open Example Parts** has three parts to open and take apart:

- **Plate with Holes, Fillets and a Chamfer**: a plate with a pin hole cut from a sketch on its top face, two filleted corners and a chamfered edge.
- **Bracket with Ribs**: an L-bracket braced by two ribs, each grown from a sketched line.
- **Flanged Pipe on a Manifold**: an advanced five-body assembly: a manifold block, an O-ring, a flange, a pipe swept along a curved path and four threaded bolts, each body in its own colour.

Select a feature in the timeline to see how it was made, and change a parameter to see the part rebuild.
