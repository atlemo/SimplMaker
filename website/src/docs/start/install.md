## What you need

- A Mac with **Apple silicon** (M1 or later)
- **macOS 26** or later
- A trackpad or a mouse. A trackpad is nicer for orbiting.

SimplMaker works offline. It needs the internet only to activate a licence key, to check that key about once a week, and to look for updates.

## Install

1. [Download SimplMaker](/download/). You get a disk image called `SimplMaker.dmg`.
2. Open it and drag **SimplMaker** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open SimplMaker from Applications. It is signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens without a warning.

The first thing you see is the [welcome window](doc:start/examples-and-tutorial).

## The free trial

For **14 days** you can use all of SimplMaker, with no account and no card. The trial starts the first time you open the app.

When it ends you can still **open** your parts and **export** them as STEP or STL. You cannot change them until you enter a licence key. A banner at the top of the window tells you how many days are left, or that editing is off.

## Buy and activate

1. Buy a licence on the [pricing page](/pricing/). A key arrives by email.
2. In SimplMaker, choose **SimplMaker → License…**.
3. Paste the key and press **Activate**.

That's it. There is no account and no sign-in.

:::note
One key works on **two Macs**. To move it to a third, open **SimplMaker → License…** on one of the first two and press **Deactivate This Mac**. That frees the activation.
:::

### How the licence is checked

After you activate, SimplMaker asks the licence server about once a week whether the key is still valid. If you are offline it keeps working for 30 days before it needs to ask again. Your parts are never sent anywhere.

A key covers **one major version** (all of 1.x, for example) and never expires. See [Licence and activation](doc:reference/license) for the details.

## Updates

SimplMaker looks for updates by itself. To check now, choose **SimplMaker → Check for Updates…**. To turn the automatic check off, open **Settings → General**. What changed in each version is in the [changelog](/changelog/).
