Questions.

How does the trial work?

Download the app and use all of it for {{trial_days}} days. No card, no account. After that you can still open your parts and export them, but you can't change them until you enter a licence key.

Do I need an account?

No. After you buy, a licence key arrives by email. You type it into SimplMaker once. The app only contacts the licence server to activate and about once a week to check the key, and it keeps working offline in between.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Yes, a key works on two Macs. To move it, choose Deactivate This Mac in SimplMaker, then activate on the new one.

Is there a Windows version?

No. SimplMaker is built natively for Mac (macOS 26 or later, Apple Silicon), and that's where it will stay for now.

What if I stop updating?

Nothing happens. Your version keeps working. Your parts are plain files you own, and you can export them as STEP at any time.

Who owns what I make?

You do. Your parts are plain files that stay on your Mac, to print, sell or share as you like. The software licence is for you and your own Macs; the details are in the licence agreement.

How do I pay, and does the price include tax?

Checkout is handled by Polar, which acts as the seller of record and takes care of sales tax and VAT. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay. Questions about an order: {{support_email}}. See also refunds.

I bought version 1. Will I get a discount on 2?

Yes. Owners of version 1 will get a discount code by email when version 2 launches.
