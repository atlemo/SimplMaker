Support

Ask me.

I answer the email myself. A real human, with a real keyboard.

{{support_email}}

Include, if you can

  • Your SimplMaker version (SimplMaker menu → About)
  • Your macOS version
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot or the part file helps a lot.

Licence problems

  • Key not accepted: check it is for this major version, and that it has no stray spaces.
  • Moving to a new Mac: choose Deactivate This Mac on the old one first. A key works on two Macs.
  • Lost your key: write to me from the email you bought with. It happens to everyone.
