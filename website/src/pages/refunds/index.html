Legal

Refunds

Last updated 8 October 2026.

Try it first

You can use the whole of SimplMaker free for 14 days, with no account and no card, before you pay anything. That trial is how you decide whether SimplMaker is right for you and your Mac. Because of it, and because your licence key is delivered digitally and works at once, we do not offer a change-of-mind refund after you have bought.

When we do put things right

Write to {{support_email}} from the email address you bought with, and we will fix it or refund you, if:

  • you were charged twice, or by mistake;
  • you bought the wrong major version;
  • your key does not work and we cannot make it work;
  • SimplMaker has a fault that stops you using it as described on this website, and we cannot fix it for you in reasonable time.

A refund cancels the licence key. Refunds are paid back to the payment method you used, by Polar, which is the seller of record. They can take a few days to show up.

Your legal rights

Nothing on this page takes away rights you have by law. In the EU/EEA, the UK and some other places, consumers may have a right to withdraw from a distance purchase within 14 days. For digital content that is delivered at once, that right can end once you have asked for delivery to start straight away and acknowledged that you lose it. You also have statutory rights if what you bought is faulty. To use any of these rights, write to us or to Polar and we will help.

How to ask

Email {{support_email}} with the email address you bought with and what went wrong. Keep your order receipt from Polar; it also has a link to Polar's own support.
