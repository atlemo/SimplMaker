Features

What it
does.

A focused CAD tool: sketch, shape, finish, export. Every value can be a number or a formula, so change one number and the model rebuilds.

01

Parameters
drive it all.

Define width, wall or tolerance once and use them anywhere. Values take units and maths: depth / 3, 1 in, hole_d + 0.2 mm.

  • Named parameters, shared across the part
  • Units: mm, cm, in and more
  • Autocomplete in every field
{{clip:parameters|1728/1080|Clip · edit the width parameter, the sketch, extrusion and fillet all follow}}

02

Sketch with
constraints.

Lines, arcs, circles, rectangles, splines and points, held in place by dimensions and constraints, which are rules like "these two lines stay parallel". Drag a corner and the sketch moves as far as the rules allow.

  • Coincident, parallel, perpendicular, tangent, equal, concentric and more
  • Trim, extend, offset, corner fillet
  • A live count of what can still move (degrees of freedom)
{{clip:sketch|1728/1080|Clip · draw a bracket profile, add dimensions, drag a corner to see the solver work}}

03

Pull it
into 3D.

Extrude a sketch up, revolve it around an axis, sweep it along a path or loft between shapes. Rib and pipe are there too, all with a drag handle and a typed value. Start from a box, cylinder, sphere, torus or coil when that is quicker.

  • One direction, two directions or mirrored
  • New body, join, cut or intersect
  • Move, rotate and scale bodies and sketches
{{clip:extrude|1728/1080|Clip · select a profile, drag the extrude arrow, switch between join and cut}}

04

Round the edges,
drill the holes.

Round edges off with a fillet, bevel them with a chamfer, shell a body hollow, drill holes and cut threads. Features refer to faces and edges by stable names, so they stay put when you change a size earlier in the timeline.

  • Fillet, chamfer (equal, distance and angle, two distances)
  • Shell, hole, thread
  • Body colours that save with the part
{{clip:finish|1728/1080|Clip · fillet the edges, shell it hollow, add a threaded hole}}

05

A timeline
you can edit.

Every step is a feature in the timeline. Click one to edit it, switch it off, or roll back and change an earlier sketch. If a feature fails, the ones after it still rebuild and the problem is marked.

  • Edit any sketch or feature later
  • Switch steps off (suppress) or delete them
  • Errors explain themselves on hover
{{clip:timeline|1728/1080|Clip · roll back the timeline, edit an early sketch, everything after rebuilds}}

06

In from STEP.
Out to STL.

Import a STEP file, a common format for sharing CAD models, and keep modelling on it: fillet it, cut it, move it. Export your bodies as STL or 3MF for the slicer, or as STEP for any other CAD tool. Sketches go in and out as DXF.

  • Import STEP, STL and DXF; export STEP, STL, 3MF and DXF
  • Imported files are stored inside the part
  • Local files, no cloud, and STEP out when you want to leave
{{clip:export|1728/1080|Clip · import a STEP file and keep modelling on it}}

07

Too big?
Cut it down.

The Split workspace cuts a part that is bigger than your printer into pieces that fit, with pins, keys, dovetails, dowels or magnets so they go back together.

  • Printer presets, or your own build volume
  • Even cuts, the fewest pieces, and a fit you can tune
  • Pieces exported laid out on plates, ready to slice
  • Change the body and the pieces follow
{{clip:cut|3446/2160|Clip · pick a printer, cut a big part into pieces, add connectors}}

08

Make it once.
Cast it often.

The Mold workspace wraps a printable mold round any body, so you can cast copies in silicone or resin. It is marked experimental, so print a small test mold first.

  • Two or four pieces, with a gate, vents and keys
  • A release check that shows undercuts before you pour
  • The silicone to mix, worked out for you
  • Change the body and the mold follows
{{clip:mold|3446/2160|Clip · pick a body, make a mold around it, check the undercuts, open it up}}

09

Properly
a Mac app.

A native Mac app: parts open as tabs, with autosave and versions, light and dark mode, trackpad gestures and a 3D view that runs on Apple's Metal. It works offline.

  • Built for Apple Silicon
  • Autosave, versions and Open Recent
  • No browser tab, no upload, no staring at a spinner
