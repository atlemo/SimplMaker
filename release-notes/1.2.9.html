SimplMaker 1.2.9

Too big to print? Too good to have just one? Two new workspaces, and a front door to match.

  • Split to Print: a part bigger than your printer gets cut into pieces that fit, with pins, keys, dovetails, dowels or magnets so they find each other again. Pick your printer, choose a fit, press Export. The pieces arrive laid out on plates, ready to slice.
  • Mold (still labelled experimental, so go easy on it): wrap a printable mould round any body and cast copies in silicone or resin. Two pieces or four, with a gate, vents and keys, and a Release check that paints every undercut red before the silicone finds it. Your AI assistant can make molds too.
  • Workspaces: Start, Design, Split, Mold and Render now live in one list under a big button at the top left. Render gets its own tool row, with quality, turntable and export in reach.
  • A proper Start page replaces the welcome window: new and recent parts as picture cards (the last 20), the tutorial, the demo part, and the video tutorials.
  • Tabs are back, slim and flat under the title bar, with a little picture of each part. Every part opens as a tab, even from the Finder.
  • Liquid Glass panels on macOS 26, with a Panel style and Panel material in Settings if you prefer the old frosting.
  • Draft lets you click the face that stays put first, and a Stays put menu and a flip button for changing your mind. Failed drafts now say which angle would have worked.
  • Offset follows splines and ellipses: move the original and its offset comes along.
