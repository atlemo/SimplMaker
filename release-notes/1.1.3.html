SimplMaker 1.1.3

Move can turn about any point, circles can be projected into a sketch, and the sketch menus are clearer.

  • Move: the turn can be about a point of your choosing instead of the middle of what moves. The Inspector has a Pivot menu (the origin, and the centres and corners of what moves) and Pivot X, Y and Z fields. The manipulator sits on the pivot.
  • Project Edge: circles and arcs that face the sketch can be projected, not only straight edges. A circular edge seen straight on comes in with a fixed centre and a construction circle or arc, and follows the body when it is rebuilt.
  • Sketch Pattern: clicking a copy in a pattern now only selects it, so copies can be constrained and dimensioned. To change the pattern, press the button at the top right of its originals. The button stays visible when the constraint symbols are hidden.
  • Constrain menu: each constraint has an icon and a tooltip, and the ones that do not fit the selection are faded.
  • Chamfer and fillet: a body that already has hair-thin faults, for example from a circular pattern whose copies miss each other by a hair, can now be chamfered or rounded. Before, every size failed with "produced an invalid solid".
  • Chamfer: Two Distances and Distance and Angle are faster on bodies with many edges, and the corner check before a chamfer is quicker.
