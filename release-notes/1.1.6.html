SimplMaker 1.1.6

Materials for the Render view: metal, glass and wood. Fillets and chamfers get tougher, and tell you why when they can't build.

  • Materials: pick Metal, Glass or Wood in the Appearance panel. A material is saved in the part, shows in the Render view and goes along when you copy and paste a body.
  • Render view: the button is a camera now, and the browser and Inspector close while the view is on, so the part gets the whole window.
  • Fillet: more rounds build, including creases that fade into a shallow angle and rounds after a thin cut through the part.
  • Fillet and chamfer no longer crash the app if the kernel falters. You get an error message, and the part stays as it was.
  • An outline of many short straight lines (a traced curve) can't be rounded at any size. The error now says so: draw it as a spline or arcs instead.
  • Assistant: sketches can have a spline, so an organic outline is one smooth curve.
  • Timeline: the right-click menus are quick again on long timelines.
