SimplMaker 1.3.0

Planes that know where they are going, and sweeps that finally know how to twist.

  • Plane Along Path (⇧N): click a sketch curve and a plane stands square to it. Drag the dot along the path, or type a position from 0 to 1, and watch the plane follow before you press OK. It is the quickest way to get a profile plane for a sweep or a section plane for a loft.
  • Plane at Angle (⇧R): turn a plane or a flat face by an angle about an axis or an edge. Click the base, click the edge to turn about (or choose X, Y or Z), type the angle. Use axis direction only projects the axis onto the base and pivots about its origin, or a point you click, which is what an angled bracket wants.
  • Twist on Sweep: the profile can turn about the path as it travels along it, on straight and curved paths alike. A rope, a twisted bar, a ring of strands that spiral. Type the total twist in the Sweep's Inspector (a parameter works too), and your AI assistant can set it with create_sweep.
  • Sweeps of profiles that touch: a ring of circles that just kiss each other used to end in Sweep produced an invalid solid. They now sweep as the separate strands they are.
  • Constraints on pattern copies: Tangent (and the other constraints) on a copy made by a Pattern now holds, by moving the original. Make a ring of circles tangent in one click instead of arguing with the numbers.
  • Planes tidy up after themselves: leave a sketch you drew on a construction plane and the plane hides. The eye in the browser brings it back.
  • Shell tries again in Intersection mode when the plain offset fails, which rescues shells on rounded lofts.
  • Start page polish, and a Support this project button in the title bar.
