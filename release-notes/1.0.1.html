SimplMaker 1.0.1

Fixes and small improvements since 1.0. The kind of tidying up you do right after a party.

  • A new document no longer jumps when its first body is extruded. The view stays right where it was.
  • Scrolling, the mouse wheel and pinch zoom toward the cursor, so the part under the pointer stays under it.
  • Projection: dragging the height past zero makes a deboss (a cut into the face).
  • Smooth curve runs extrude to one face, and value fields no longer pick up the dragger.
  • The Project tool has a new toolbar icon.
  • The cursor prompt stays hidden over the toolbar and side panels.
  • The AI picture is never returned half-size anymore.
