SimplMaker 1.2.1

The Render view grew up. It can now look like a photograph, if you ask it to, and it still looks like before if you would rather it didn't.

  • Quality presets: Simple (the look you know), Realistic and Photo. They switch on physical light with a filmic curve, ambient occlusion, contact shadows, a softbox overhead, a little glow and darker corners, and glass that bends the floor behind it. A new installation starts at Realistic; your saved settings stay on Simple until you choose.
  • The Render options have two tabs. Simple holds what an everyday render needs, Advanced holds every setting a preset sets, one by one, plus exposure, finish, depth blur and noise. Five rooms to reflect: Studio, Light tent, Overcast, Sunset and Dark studio.
  • New material: Plastic, with a Roughness slider from glossy to matte. Metal got the same slider, from polished to brushed. Roughness is saved in the file.
  • Depth blur has a Select focus point button: click the spot that should be sharp. No preset turns depth blur on.
  • Export render can make a picture from 16 or 64 slightly shifted ones and average them, for smooth edges and soft effects without grain.
  • The Render panel now floats above the status bar, and the timeline is put away along with the browser and Inspector while you render. A selected body no longer gets an outline over its material.
  • Glass that touches another body (a peg on a face, a lid on a base) no longer shimmers with bright crescents where the two meet.
