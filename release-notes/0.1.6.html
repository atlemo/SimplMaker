SimplMaker 0.1.6 (beta)

  • AI assistants can now work on your part: connect Claude Desktop or Claude Code in one click from Settings, and let it build, change, check and look at a part, with an activity log of what it did. Off until you turn it on.
  • Browser: make your own folders inside Bodies, nest them, and export a whole folder as one STL or STEP file.
  • Toolbar: tools sit next to the title and use the room on the right, parts open as tabs, and the toolbar text follows the sketch tint by contrast.
  • Rib: works with a line in either direction, goes on the body it reaches without a target, and says which end of the line is wrong.
  • Timeline: a new sketch goes in at the marker, and editing a rolled-back sketch brings it back.
  • Sketch edits are several times faster; the grid no longer shows through bodies; arrow keys nudge a number in the Parameters value field.
