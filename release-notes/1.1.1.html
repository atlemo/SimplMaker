SimplMaker 1.1.1

Fixes for angle dimensions, chamfers and fillets, plus a few sketching and viewing improvements.

  • Angle dimensions: clicking the "click to place" label no longer opens an editor that ignored what you typed. Typing a parameter name (like angle) keeps the dimension linked to it instead of storing its number.
  • Arcs: a sweep typed while drawing an arc becomes a dimension, so it can follow a parameter, and can be changed later.
  • Chamfer and Fillet: a new one that is too big for its edges now starts at a size that fits (half, a quarter...) with its arrow ready to adjust, instead of failing.
  • Chamfers and fillets now work across the hair-thin faces two rounds leave when they use up a whole wall (1.5 mm rounds on a 3 mm wall). When a size still does not fit, the message says which part of the edge is too short.
  • Sketching: a new Show Shape Centres option in the Inspector marks the middle of every closed shape with an X.
  • Revolve: the angle is a dot on the body's rim, instead of a long arrow that put its field far from the part.
  • The ground grid fades as the view flattens, so it no longer shimmers when seen almost edge-on.
  • Help menu: a link to the SimplMaker documentation.
