SimplMaker 1.2.5

Sketches got a bigger toolbox, lofts can follow a path, and rounded rectangles finally keep their size when you change the radius.

  • More ways to draw: Midpoint Line, 3-Point and Center Rounded Rectangle, 3-Point, 2-Tangent and 3-Tangent Circle (two parallel lines give a 2-Tangent circle that fills the gap), Circumscribed and Edge Polygon, Center Point, 3-Point and Center Arc Slot, and Control Point Spline. They all live in one Create group now: pin the ones you use on the strip, find the rest in the list with its hover submenus.
  • Loft along a path: give a Loft a path (an open chain of sketch curves from the first profile's plane to the last) and the shape follows it. Where the path meets every profile square, the shape turns with the path; otherwise the profiles have to be parallel. Pick it with the Loft tool or in the Inspector. For AI assistants, create_loft takes path_sketch and path.
  • See the other sketches while you sketch: the sketches you have already made are drawn faintly behind the one you are editing, and the cursor snaps to their points and curves. Show Other Sketches in the sketch's Inspector turns it off.
  • Intersect and Project Again: Intersect (Q) brings in the curves where the sketch plane cuts a body, and they follow the body when it changes. Project Edge also takes curves of earlier sketches. If a projected edge goes away, select the projection and use Project Again (W) to point it at a new edge of the same kind; its points and constraints stay. Clean Up Projections removes the ones nothing uses, and Clean Up Stale Projections the ones whose edge is gone.
  • Rounded rectangles keep their size: changing the corner radius used to make the whole rectangle grow or shrink, depending on which corner you started from. Now only the corners change. Clicking the grey radius of another corner edits the one radius they all share, instead of adding a second dimension that fights it.
  • Cleaner section view: where two bodies touch, the cut face no longer lets grey blocks of the neighbour show through the hatching.
  • The Top and Bottom views look exactly straight down, so you no longer see a sliver of the side walls.
