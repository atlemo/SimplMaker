SimplMaker 1.2.7

A Space bar that does something useful, a tidier right-hand side, and STEP files that stop insisting their flat faces are not flat.

  • Sketch shortcuts panel: press Space while you sketch and a small panel floats up with the tools you use most. Click one to pick it, or type in the search field to reach any sketch tool. Customize lets you choose which tools it shows. Drag it wherever you like and it remembers the spot. Prefer another key? Sketch Shortcuts Panel in Settings, under Shortcuts, takes any single key (the Space bar will not be offended).
  • Appearance has moved in: the right-hand side is now two foldable panels, Inspector on top and Appearance below it, built like the Browser and Parameters on the left. Drag the divider to give either one more room, double-click it to even them out.
  • Flat faces from other CAD programs: many STEP files store a perfectly flat face as a free-form surface, and SimplMaker answered with "face is not planar". A face now counts as flat when it lies on one plane to within a micrometre, so you can sketch on it, extrude it and drill into it.
  • Constraint symbols behave when you zoom out: the little symbols on arcs and circles hide when the view is too far out to read them, and crowded curves get more room before they show.
  • See-through bodies show the right amount of grid: a body at 97 % opacity used to hide the grid completely. It now shows through by exactly what the slider leaves, and nothing is outlined as selected while you drag the slider.
