SimplMaker 0.1.7 (beta)

  • Mirror: mirror sketch shapes across a line (the copy stays linked), and mirror bodies along an axis or a straight edge.
  • Extrude: choose which bodies a Join, Cut or Intersect affects.
  • Lock bodies and sketches so they cannot be changed by accident. While you sketch, faces are lit evenly and Slice View cuts away what is in front of the sketch plane.
  • Hole: the depth and diameter arrows and the move dot now show on every hole, also when cutting merges the hole's face with a neighbour.
  • Chamfer and Fillet: a round that is too big for its corner or its face is refused with a message instead of giving a wrong shape.
  • Sketch: short offset dimensions stay visible, and mirrored shapes hold their points on the mirror line.
  • Browser and Parameters: the selected row uses the accent colour, Tab moves between parameter values, and the picture glides to the new centre when a panel is shown or hidden.
