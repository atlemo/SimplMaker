SimplMaker 1.2.8

Colour inside the lines, or deliberately outside them.

  • Colour single faces: switch the Appearance panel to Faces and give a face its own colour, material and roughness. Red button, grey box, no paintbrush.
  • 3MF keeps face colours, so your slicer sees the red button too.
  • Opacity works in the Render view, with shadows and reflections to match.
  • Sketches open framed: no more hunting for a sketch that wandered off camera.
  • Sketch shortcuts panel lets go of the mouse: pan, zoom and orbit work while it is open.
  • Tidier panels: buttons line up on one grid, and Appearance starts open like its siblings.
  • Help links to the video tutorials, and there is a Constraints Sampler example part to poke at.
