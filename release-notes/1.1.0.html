SimplMaker 1.1.0

More sketch tools, and a way to pull body edges into a sketch.

  • Project Edge (sketch, key U): click a straight edge of a body to bring it into the sketch, even one on the far side of the part. It stays linked to the body, so dimensions and constraints follow along when the body changes.
  • New sketch tools: Slot and Overall Slot, Rounded Rectangle, Tangent Arc, Ellipse, and a sketch Chamfer.
  • Placed angles stay on their wedge, and can be dragged along it.
  • Sketch Dimension and Distance have swapped keys, and the shortcut reset now labels each key. Sorry for the musical chairs.
  • Trim and Extend behave better near the ends of lines and arcs.
