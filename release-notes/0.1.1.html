SimplMaker 0.1.1 (beta)

  • Copy and paste bodies (Edit ▸ Copy, Paste), within a part or between parts. A pasted body opens in Move, so you can slide it off the original before they get confusing.
  • New first-run tutorial: build a desk organizer, one step at a time. Your pencil case will thank you.
  • Drag a parameter's name left or right to change its value. Right-click to rename or delete.
  • Planar face normals fixed, so chamfer faces point outward like they should have all along. Chamfer limits at rounded corners are clearer, too.
  • Sketch mode: left-drag on empty space orbits, just like in Design mode. One less thing to remember.
  • New Measure tool, Project tool, example parts, accent colour and a pile of smaller refinements.
  • Parametric sketches, extrude, revolve, sweep, loft, fillet, chamfer, threads and more. The usual suspects are all here.
  • 14-day free trial, then a license key.
  • Automatic updates, so you never have to hunt for the newest version.
  • License activation: if a key from another major version is turned away, its activation slot is now freed reliably (and retried if the license server is having a moment).
