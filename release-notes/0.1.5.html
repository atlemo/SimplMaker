SimplMaker 0.1.5 (beta)

  • New Section Analysis tool (Inspect, ⇧A): cut your bodies with a plane, a flat face, a sketch or a canvas to peek inside, and drag the cut in or out in 1 mm steps. Turn it on and off whenever you like.
  • New tools: Split Body, Draft and Lay on Floor. Scale now works on bodies too, and Extrude can start from a face and has a taper dial.
  • FDM 3D Print Preview (View menu): shades bodies like a printed part, layer lines and perimeters included. A sneak peek before the plastic.
  • Import STL, next to STEP and SVG in File ▸ Import.
  • Browser and Parameters are now two collapsible, resizable panels. Navigation settings have presets and per-button bindings, and sketches can roll.
  • Much faster rebuilds after a parameter edit, and faster sketches with hundreds of curves. Thread fixes too.
