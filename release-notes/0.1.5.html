SimplMaker 0.1.5 (beta)

  • New Section Analysis tool (Inspect, ⇧A): cut the bodies by a plane, a flat face, a sketch or a canvas to look inside them, and drag the cut in or out in 1 mm steps. Turn it on and off at any time.
  • New tools: Split Body, Draft, Lay on Floor; Scale now works on bodies too; Extrude can start from a face and has a taper dial.
  • FDM 3D Print Preview (View menu): shades bodies like a printed part, with layer lines and perimeters.
  • Import STL next to STEP and SVG in File ▸ Import.
  • Browser and Parameters are two collapsible, resizable panels; navigation settings have presets and per-button bindings; sketch view roll.
  • Much faster rebuilds after a parameter edit, and faster sketches with hundreds of curves; thread fixes.
