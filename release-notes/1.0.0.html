SimplMaker 1.0

The first full release: SimplMaker is out of beta. Thank you to everyone who tried the betas and told us what to fix.

  • Runs on macOS 15 Sequoia and later, not just macOS 26. On macOS 15 the tools sit in a strip of their own under the toolbar.
  • STL import: meshes of up to 100,000 triangles open as they are, and bigger ones can be reduced as they are imported.
  • Sketch: with hidden edges shown, the parts of a sketch inside or behind a body show dashed, like the hidden edges.
  • Sketch: the value fields stay readable in dark mode, and the view no longer keeps redrawing after a tool's first click, so it uses less power.
  • Parameters: clicking outside the list lets go of the selected row and its field.
  • The angle dial's arc and arrows are lighter, and its readout stays clear of the dial.
