SimplMaker 1.0

The first full release: SimplMaker is out of beta. Thank you to everyone who tried the betas and told us what to fix. You know who you are, and the app is better for it.

  • Runs on macOS 15 Sequoia and later, not just macOS 26. On macOS 15 the tools sit in a strip of their own under the toolbar.
  • STL import: meshes of up to 100,000 triangles open as they are, and bigger ones can be slimmed down as they come in.
  • Sketch: with hidden edges shown, the parts of a sketch inside or behind a body show dashed, like the hidden edges.
  • Sketch: the value fields stay readable in dark mode, and the view no longer keeps redrawing after a tool's first click, so it sips less power.
  • Parameters: click outside the list and the selected row and its field let go.
  • The angle dial's arc and arrows are lighter, and its readout stays clear of the dial.
