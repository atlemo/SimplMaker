SimplMaker 1.1.2

Text is much quicker to work with, and threads are more flexible.

  • Text: click any letter of a text to pick all of it, so one click is enough to extrude, cut or project a text. Hovering lights up the whole text. ⌥-click picks a single letter, and ⇧-click adds or removes a whole text. The insides of letters such as O and B are left out, so extruding a text does not fill them.
  • Text: the letters now appear in the sketch as you type them, at the size and place they will have, instead of after you press Return. The same goes for changing the words of a text.
  • Text: a Size field sits beside the words. Press Tab to move to it and type a length, or a parameter, and the letters change as you type. A new text starts with the size of the last one.
  • Text: extruding or cutting a text is faster. Letters that only have overlapping boxes, like the two f's of "Offset", no longer make the whole line of text go through a merge.
  • Thread: any round face can be threaded. When no standard size suits it, the thread is a Custom one, with the diameter and pitch you type.
  • Thread: holes and shafts of different sizes picked together are made as one step. The tolerance offset, length and direction apply to all of them, and each has its own size. A hole drawn at a thread's own size (Ø5) takes that size (M5).
  • Thread: threads build about three times faster, and so does what comes after them.
  • Thread: the tolerance presets are twice as large: PETG 2 %, ASA 3 %, ABS 4 %, Nylon 6 %.
  • Sketch: the X and Y axes can be picked as lines in constraints (Symmetric, Coincident, Parallel, Perpendicular, Tangent).
  • Sketch Pattern: pressing Pattern on curves that are already patterned changes that pattern, instead of starting a new one.
  • View cube: the arrows that turn a sketch a quarter turn are larger, sit round the cube and light up when hovered.
  • Quitting while a rebuild is running no longer crashes.
