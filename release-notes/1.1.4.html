SimplMaker 1.1.4

A Render view for presentation: a solid floor, a light you set, soft shadows and a reflection.

  • Render view: a new Render button at the right end of the toolbar (also View ▸ Render and the viewport's right-click menu) shows the part on a solid floor instead of the grid, without edge lines, as a picture. It is a view setting and is not saved in the part.
  • Floor: six colours, and a reflection of the part in it with its own strength and a Blur slider. The reflection stays sharp where the part touches the floor and softens with height.
  • Light: Studio, Soft and Dramatic to start from, and a dial for the direction, with Brightness, Height and Ambient sliders. The light stays where you put it in the world, so turning the view changes how the part is lit.
  • Shadows: the part casts soft shadows on the floor and on itself. They are sharp where they start and softer the farther they fall, and a Softness slider sets how soft.
  • The Render panel: every slider shows its value, a double-click puts a control back, Reset puts back all of them, and the panel follows the Render button. View ▸ Render Options hides just the panel for a clean screenshot. The settings are kept between launches.
