SimplMaker 1.2.6

See-through bodies, a Select filter, a Concave fillet, and sketches that finally take their bodies along when you move them.

  • Opacity: the Appearance panel has an Opacity slider. Turn a body down and you can see through it in the normal view, with the grid, other bodies and their edges showing behind it. It saves with the file, can be undone, and copy and paste keeps it.
  • Select filter: the arrow beside Select picks what it takes: everything, Points only or Curves only. With Points, a click reaches the end of a line lying over it and a box takes every point. A click on a kind the filter skips reaches what is beneath.
  • Concave fillet: a new option on Fillet. On a convex edge it cuts a scoop, in an inside corner it adds a bead, in both cases with the arc centred on the edge.
  • Move or Scale a sketch, and what was made from it comes along: before, bodies built from the sketch stayed where they were, so one sketch could give shapes of different sizes. Now they follow. The Inspector offers Everything from this sketch (the default) or Only later features. Files from before keep the old behaviour.
  • Lay on Floor takes the other bodies in the picked body's folder along, so a part and its friends turn and drop together.
  • Add as parameter: a button in the sketch size box and the dimension popover turns a number into a named parameter in one click. A parameter's name is a proper field now, and one click renames it.
  • Midpoint on two lines lines their middles up (a construction line, kept vertical or horizontal, does the holding).
  • Smaller things: the single-key tool shortcut goes to the way of drawing you used last, dimension labels let your clicks through while a draw tool is active, the Dimension tool starts with a single selected line already picked, and the Inspector says "Add Constraint (n selected)" and selects what a constraint row holds when you click it.
  • Tools that are waiting for you (Combine, Chamfer and friends) show what they need in the Inspector, with Cancel and Create, instead of "No Feature Selected". Fillet and Chamfer say so, and keep waiting, when a pick no longer names an edge.
  • ⌥-drag on empty space turns the sketch on its paper again.
