SimplMaker 1.1.5

Take your part apart without a screwdriver: an Exploded view. You can also select several bodies at once, and dragging a handle on a zoomed-out part stays gentle.

  • Exploded view: View ▸ Exploded View (or the viewport's right-click menu) pulls the bodies of a part apart with one slider, in equal steps, along X, Y, Z or all three, to show how it goes together. A view setting only, so nothing in the part moves, and it pairs nicely with the Render view for a picture. Assembly instructions, minus the Allen key.
  • Browser: ⌘-click or ⇧-click selects several bodies. Right-click them (or use the Inspector) to move or rotate, scale, recolour, hide, show, lock, put in a folder or export them together. Select the bodies first, then choose the tool.
  • Handles: dragging a length handle on a zoomed-out view no longer jumps in big steps. A step is never more than 1 mm.
