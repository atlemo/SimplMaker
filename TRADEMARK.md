# The name and the icon

The code of SimplMaker is free software under the GPL (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The **name SimplMaker** and the **app icon**
(`Packaging/AppIcon.*` and `Packaging/DocIcon.*`) are not part of that licence. They identify the version of the app that
Atle Mo publishes, and the project asks that they keep doing so. The name is not a registered trademark, but it is used as one.

What you may do:

- Build and use SimplMaker from this source, for yourself or for others, unchanged. An unmodified build can keep the name and the icon.
- Write and speak about SimplMaker, link to it, and say that your tool or file works with it.
- Fork the code and publish your changes under the GPL, as long as the result is not confused with the original.

What we ask of a modified version that you publish or distribute:

- Give it **another name** and **another icon**. "Based on SimplMaker" is a fine thing to say, in plain text.
- Do not use the SimplMaker name, the icon, or the simplmaker.com address in a way that suggests that we made, approved or support your version.
- Do not point your builds at our update feed. They would not accept our updates anyway (the archives are signed with a key you do not have),
  and a build you make yourself has no feed unless you set one: see `scripts/bundle-app.sh`.

Questions: support@simplmaker.com.
